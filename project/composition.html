Composition Rules That Help Beginners

Great composition makes your subject feel intentional. These easy-to-use rules help your eye find the best placement for people, objects, and landscapes.

Rule of Thirds

Imagine your frame divided into nine equal boxes. Place the most important element on one of the lines or intersections to create balance and interest.

  • Put the horizon on the top or bottom third instead of in the center.
  • Place a person’s eyes near an upper intersection point.

Leading Lines

Use roads, fences, shadows, or rows of trees to guide the viewer’s eye to your subject.

Leading lines create depth and make your photo feel more dynamic.

Framing

Frame the subject using natural elements like doorways, windows, tree branches, or archways. Framing draws attention and provides context.

Simplify the Background

A clean background helps your subject stand out. Move slightly until clutter is minimized or use a shallow depth effect on your phone to blur the background.
