Editing Tips

Editing helps your phone photos look cleaner, brighter, and more polished.

Crop and Straighten

Remove distractions by cropping and make your horizon level for a polished look.

Adjust Exposure

Brighten or darken the photo to keep the subject visible but avoid blowing out highlights.

Boost Contrast Carefully

Contrast helps details pop, but too much can make the photo look harsh.

Use Saturation with Restraint

Increase color slightly for life, then reduce it if the photo begins to feel fake.

Try an Editing Routine

  1. Crop and straighten
  2. Adjust brightness/exposure
  3. Increase contrast slightly
  4. Fine-tune color and sharpness
