Camera Basics

These three settings help you control exposure and create the look you want.

Aperture

Aperture controls how much light enters the lens and how much of the scene is in focus.

  • Lower number (f/1.8, f/2.8) = more light + blurrier background.
  • Higher number (f/8, f/11) = less light + sharper background.

Shutter Speed

Shutter speed is how long the camera sensor sees light.

  • Fast speed freezes motion.
  • Slow speed adds motion blur and works well for waterfalls or night shots.

ISO

ISO makes the camera more sensitive to light.

  • Lower ISO = cleaner photo in bright light.
  • Higher ISO = brighter image in low light, but more noise.

Practical Rule

Start with a low ISO, set the aperture for the background look you want, then adjust shutter speed for correct brightness.
