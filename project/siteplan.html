ShutterStart

Site Plan Document – Beginner’s Guide to Photography

1. Site Name

ShutterStart

This name was chosen because it captures the exact moment a beginner takes their first intentional photograph — the press of the shutter. It feels welcoming, action-oriented, and clearly communicates that the site is about starting a photography journey. The name is short, memorable, and easy to brand.

Optional domain idea: shutterstart.guide or shutterstart.photo

2. Site Purpose

ShutterStart is an educational website designed for absolute beginners who want to improve their photography skills. The site provides clear, friendly, and practical information about:

  • Taking better photos with a smartphone
  • Understanding basic camera settings (aperture, shutter speed, ISO)
  • Composition rules and creative techniques
  • Simple photo editing using free apps
  • Recommended beginner-friendly resources and gear

The overall goal is to make photography feel approachable and fun rather than technical or intimidating.

3. Scenarios

These are questions that a typical visitor (a beginner photographer) might ask when they arrive at the site:

Scenario 1

“I only have my phone. How can I take better photos without buying a camera?”

→ This drives the need for a dedicated Smartphone Photography page with practical tips.

Scenario 2

“What are the most important camera settings I should learn first as a complete beginner?”

→ This supports the Camera Basics section explaining aperture, shutter speed, and ISO in simple language.

Scenario 3 (bonus)

“Which free apps can I use to edit my photos and make them look more professional?”

→ This informs the Editing Tips page and resource recommendations.

4. Color Schema

The color palette is modern, clean, and slightly bold — reflecting creativity while remaining easy on the eyes for long reading sessions.

  • #1A1A2E (Deep Navy) — Used for main headings, navigation bar, footer background, and logo text.
  • #E94560 (Vibrant Coral) — Used for accent elements: buttons, links, underlines under section titles, logo background, and hover states.
  • #F7F7F9 (Soft Light Gray) — Page background color.
  • #2D2D2D (Dark Charcoal) — Body text and paragraph content.
  • #FFFFFF (White) — Card backgrounds and content sections.

Note: This document itself uses the selected color schema so you can see how the colors work together.

5. Typography

Two carefully chosen Google Fonts are used to keep the design clean and readable:

Poppins (Headings)

Used for all main headings (h1, h2, h3), the logo text, and important labels. It has a modern, geometric feel that feels energetic and contemporary.

Open Sans (Body Text)

Used for paragraphs, lists, and general reading content. It is highly legible on screens and works well for longer educational text.

Usage summary:

  • Poppins → Site title, section headings, navigation, buttons
  • Open Sans → Body paragraphs, descriptions, lists, footer text
